# Reference: Ronin Bridge (threshold validators)

**Verdict: UNSAFE.**

Operational threshold failure: attackers compromised 5 of 9 validator keys (four Sky Mavis keys plus the Axie DAO key, whose signing access should have been revoked) and signed fraudulent withdrawals. The design concentrated trust and had no independent monitoring.
