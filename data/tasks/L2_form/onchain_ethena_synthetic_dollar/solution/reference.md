# Reference: Ethena USDe (delta-neutral stablecoin)

**Verdict: UNSAFE.**

Solvency depends on centralized exchanges/custodians and perpetual funding: sustained negative funding, a major exchange failure, or collateral being rehypothecated breaks backing, and redemption can be gated exactly when it is needed. This is a real trust/design exposure, not a cryptographic break.
