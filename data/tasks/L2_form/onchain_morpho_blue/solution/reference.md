# Reference: Morpho Blue

**Verdict: UNSAFE.**

Morpho Blue deliberately pushes risk to market creation: a permissionless market with a manipulable or non-standard oracle, exotic collateral, or bad LLTV can accrue bad debt, and immutable markets cannot be paused or upgraded. Oracle/oracle-adaptor risk is entirely on the curator/borrower.
